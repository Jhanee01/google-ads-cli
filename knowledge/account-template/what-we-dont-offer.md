# What we don't offer

The source of the negative keywords (playbook: [negatives](../playbook/negatives.md)).
For each item: why it is not offered, and the words people use for it (with accent-free and
misspelled forms). Copy the single words that are always negative into `alwaysNegative` in
`google-ads.rules.json`.
